Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 Nov 2026

Spark Quotes (“we”, “us”) provides a site for inspirational quotes at quotes.wearesparklab.com. This page explains what information we collect, why, and your choices.

Information we collect

  • Account data: Email address and a unique user ID (stored with Supabase) when you sign up.
  • Content you submit: Quotes, authors, categories and timestamps.
  • Saved quotes and reminder settings: The quotes you save, and the times and days you choose for reminders.
  • Your journal: If you write in the Spark Journal, your entries (reflections and intentions, and the quote an entry is about) are stored with your account. They are private to you: no other user can see them, they are never shared or published, and they are never sent to any artificial-intelligence service. As with all account data, they are held in our database, which our small team administers; we do not read them.
  • Thoughts from a quiet minute: If you are not signed in, a thought you choose to keep is stored only in your browser on that device and is not sent to us. If you are signed in, it is saved to your journal.
  • Device & logs: Basic technical info (e.g., IP, user agent) captured by our hosting provider for security/diagnostics.
  • Notifications: If you enable push notifications, a device token may be generated to deliver messages. You can disable notifications at any time in Settings or device preferences.
  • Analytics: We use Plausible Analytics (privacy-friendly, no cookies) to understand overall usage (page views, referrers). No personal profiles are created.

How we use information

  • Provide core features (show quotes, favourites, your journal, submit quotes).
  • Moderate user-submitted content.
  • Send optional reminders/notifications if you opt in.
  • Protect our service against abuse and improve reliability.
  • We never sell your data, and we never use your journal or saved quotes for advertising.

Data processors

  • Supabase – database/authentication and file storage.
  • Vercel – website hosting and logs.
  • Plausible – privacy-focused analytics (no cookies, no cross-site tracking).

Your choices

  • Access/Deletion: You can delete your account from Settings. This removes your account and associated personal data from our database, including your saved quotes, reminders and journal.
  • Journal entries: You can delete any entry yourself, at any time, from the Journal.
  • Notifications: Toggle off in the app’s Settings or device settings.
  • Submitted quotes: We may keep anonymized, approved quotes for community quality and safety (no email attached).

Children

Spark Quotes is not directed to children under 13. Do not sign up if you are under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. We’ll post changes on this page with an updated date.
